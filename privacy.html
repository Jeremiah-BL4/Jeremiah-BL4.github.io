Privacy

Last updated September 2026

What this site collects

Nothing directly. There are no cookies, no analytics or tracking scripts, no advertising, no accounts and no forms. Nothing is stored in your browser.

Third parties

  • The fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests them from Google’s servers, so Google receives your IP address and basic browser information. Google’s own privacy policy covers that.
  • The company hosting this site may keep standard server logs (IP address, the page requested and the time) for security and reliability.

If you contact me

The email link opens your own mail app, and the phone link your phone’s dialler. If you email or call, I receive your email address or phone number and whatever you say, and use it only to reply. I don’t add anyone to a mailing list or share messages with anyone else. Ask and I’ll delete our correspondence.

The WhatsApp link opens a chat with me in WhatsApp, which is run by Meta. Messages there are covered by WhatsApp’s own privacy policy, and I receive your number and name as WhatsApp shows them.
