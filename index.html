The lab

Security and systems, which I’m currently learning.

I use Linux day to day and keep a Kali VM for practice. Most of it is fundamentals: how networks route traffic, what a running service exposes, and how to script repetitive work in Bash and Python.

It feeds back into the web work. Knowing how a form, a session or a header can be abused makes it easier to build them properly.

I’m early in this and don’t do security work professionally. All practice happens on my own machines or on platforms made for it.

About

I got into this the way a lot of people do: I wanted to change something on a page, opened the code to see how it worked, and kept going.

Most of my time goes into front-end work: building sites that load fast, read well on a phone and hold up when someone resizes the window. I like the part where a business explains what they need and I turn it into something they can use.

Outside of that I’m usually in Blender, writing a Python script to avoid doing something twice, or in a VM working through security fundamentals. I use AI tools as part of how I build, mainly for scaffolding and review, and I check what they produce.

Currently

  • Taking on freelance website builds for small businesses
  • Working through networking and Linux fundamentals
  • Getting deeper into React and modern front-end tooling
  • Rendering personal Blender projects between builds
