The place

Eat outside, under the trees.

Roast & Simmer is an open-air kitchen and bar in Buwate. The tables sit out among the trees, the grill runs on charcoal, and the pots go on slow.

Come early, while the light still comes through the branches. Or come late, when the lights are strung on and the garden fills up.

The kitchen

The name is the whole menu.

Everything comes off the coals or out of a pot. Two ways of cooking, one fire, and not much else to explain.

01 — Roast

From the coals

Pork and goat on skewers, whole chickens opened flat, ribs glazed dark and scattered with sesame. Cooked over charcoal in the open, close enough to the tables that you smell it before you order.

02 — Simmer

From the pot

Stews left long enough for the sauce to thicken — onion, tomato, carrot and green pepper, coriander cut over the top at the end. They arrive in the tray they were cooked in, for the table to work through together.

The full food menu is at the restaurant, and it changes with what is on the fire. Message us and we will tell you what is cooking today — and what we can cook for your event, since we cater outside as well.
